### 1. Establish the Sector Baseline
Upon entering any new hallway or classroom, pause for three to five seconds. Take note of ambient light fixtures, the alignment of desks, clock hands, and background audio frequencies. Memorizing this initial state prevents second-guessing later.

### 2. Execute a Grid Sweep
Divide the room into three visual planes: ceiling, eye level, and floor level. Many subtle shifts hide in overhead rafters or floorboard seams where players rarely aim their cameras.

### 3. Cross-Reference Anomaly Loggers
Compare observed environmental markers against your journal or portable detection tool. If even one poster has shifted position or changed colors, the sector is anomalous.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What constitutes a jujutsu school anomaly during standard gameplay?
A **jujutsu school anomaly** is any deliberate variation from the academy's baseline environment caused by cursed energy interference. This includes shifting architecture, altered text or portraits, anomalous audio cues, floating artifacts, and hostile cursed manifestations that require detection or purification to progress.

### How do I know if a room contains an anomaly or just background scenery?
Every sector has a fixed baseline template. If an object is inverted, a poster displays different imagery, audio channels drop out, or door placements change, an anomaly is present. When in doubt, utilize tools like Spectral Spectacles or cross-check baseline reference photos.

### What happens if I miss a subtle anomaly and proceed forward?
Failing to flag an anomaly resets your floor streak or loops you back to the beginning of the wing. Additionally, community reports show that failed sweeps increase sector corruption meters, making subsequent floors significantly more hazardous.

### Can multiple anomalies spawn in the same hallway?
Yes. On advanced difficulty levels, multi-anomaly events occur frequently. In these situations, cataloging or cleansing the highest-tier threat first ensures the barrier stabilizes without forcing an instant sector wipe.
