### Portrait Discrepancies and Biological Tells

- **Pupil Distortion:** Look closely at the irises. Anomalous entities often generate slit pupils, double irises, or uneven pupil dilation that contradicts natural human physiology.

- **Shadow Vectors:** The headshot's background shadow must fall to the subject's lower-right quadrant. Flat portraits with zero cast shadows indicate an entity projecting an illusion onto blank laminated plastic.
