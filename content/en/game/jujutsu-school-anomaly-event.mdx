## Anomaly Phases and Combat Encounter Breakdown

Every instance of the jujutsu school anomaly event follows a structured three-phase progression. Recognizing the transition cues between these phases allows squads to rotate defensive cooldowns and position themselves before devastating domain attacks unfold.

### Phase 1: Perimeter Breach and Barrier Stabilizers

The encounter initiates with the emergence of fractured cursed familiars across the exterior grounds. During this phase, two to three unaligned barrier anchors materialize at opposing corners of the campus. 

Teams must divide tasks: high-mobility players should secure the outlying anchors while dedicated frontline combatants keep wave spawns off the channeling zones. Failing to stabilize at least two anchors within the first two minutes immediately accelerates the corruption gauge.

### Phase 2: Infiltration of the Cursed Core

Once the exterior perimeter stabilizes, the core rupture draws players into the main assembly hall or subterranean training vaults. Elite cursed entities emerge here, equipped with hyper-armor and localized domain amplification fields.

Community reports highlight that elite spirits in this phase heavily punish grouped formations. Spacing of at least eight yards is recommended to prevent chained splash damage from cleave attacks.

### Phase 3: The Domain Rupture Climax

The final phase features the apex anomaly entity. The boss frequently alters the arena's spatial geometry, summoning shifting void pools and deploying pseudo-domains that nullify passive defensive buffs. 

When the boss channels its ultimate catastrophe attack, players must concentrate heavy blunt or slashing damage onto its protective barrier shell. Shattering this barrier triggers a vulnerable stagger window, opening a prime opportunity to unleash high-damage burst techniques.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Jujutsu School Anomaly Event

### How often does the jujutsu school anomaly event rotate into the schedule?
The jujutsu school anomaly event typically rotates into the active server schedule on alternating weekends, featuring reset reward lockouts that refresh every Friday at midnight server time. Special mid-week mini-fractures occasionally spawn during seasonal promotions, offering doubled token drops for brief periods.

### Can solo players complete high-tier stages of the jujutsu school anomaly event?
While Tier I and Tier II stages are easily manageable for solo players with decent gear, Tier III and above feature dual-objective mechanics that make solo runs exceptionally grueling. Player experience shows that soloing Tier IV or V requires near-flawless dodge execution and specialized self-sustaining barrier builds.

### What is the fastest method to clear barrier locks during the event?
The fastest strategy involves assigning a high-mobility player equipped with movement-speed talismans to dash directly to the outer anchors as soon as the sector opens. Using knockback skills to push swarming familiars away from channeling zones allows you to stabilize nodes without having to eliminate every enemy in the wave.

### What happens if your squad wipes during an active anomaly run?
If your full squad falls during the jujutsu school anomaly event, you forfeit any incomplete phase objective rewards, but you retain any baseline materials dropped by previously defeated wave elites. You can immediately re-queue for the encounter, provided you still have event access keys remaining in your daily inventory.
