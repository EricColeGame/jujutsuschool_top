Navigating the deeper wings of the school grounds demands methodical discipline. Follow this standardized mission loop to maximize your extraction rates and item recovery:

Before entering any corridor branch, scan the exterior door frames for warning marks. If talismans outside a room are charred or turned inward, treat the room as an active anomaly node.

Deploy your dimensional anchors near main stairwells. The procedural shifts introduced in the patch can trap players on upper floors if return routes collapse.

Monitor atmospheric cues. Heavy drops in ambient temperature, backwards audio cues, and displaced furniture indicate the specific entity class roaming the sector. Log your findings in the team ledger to unlock corresponding weakness modifiers.

### Phase 4: Neutralization or Containment
