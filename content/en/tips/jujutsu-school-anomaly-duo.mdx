## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes the jujutsu school anomaly duo setup more effective than solo play?
Operating as a pair grants divided aggro, access to status-stacking combos, and insurance against sudden one-shot mechanics. In higher-tier anomaly runs, enemies feature crowd-control abilities designed to incapacitate solitary sorcerers, making cooperative intervention crucial.

### Can two players with the same archetype form an effective duo?
While double-Vanguard or double-Striker teams can clear low-grade encounters through brute force, they struggle in extended battles. Symmetrical builds suffer from identical weaknesses, such as lack of range, poor defense, or rapid energy depletion during prolonged phases.

### How should a jujutsu school anomaly duo handle enraged anomaly phases?
When an anomaly enters an enraged state, both players should prioritize defensive evasion and spacing over damage output. The primary goal during this window is conserving cursed energy, parrying incoming fury combos, and waiting for the enrage gauge to expire before re-engaging with high-impact bursts.
