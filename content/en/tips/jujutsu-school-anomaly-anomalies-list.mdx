## Advanced Settings and Spotting Strategies

Spotting minor anomalies often comes down to hardware setup and visual clarity rather than raw reflexes. Subtle changes, such as a poster's eyes shifting slightly to the left, are nearly impossible to catch if motion blur is active or screen gamma is set too dark.

Apply these configuration optimizations to maximize your perception:

* **Disable Motion Blur and Film Grain:** These cinematic settings obscure micro-distortions in wall textures and fine text.
* **Adjust Brightness (Gamma):** Raise brightness slightly above default. Shadows conceal misplaced props and altered floor markings.
* **Wear Stereo Headphones:** Many anomalies trigger directional sound cues—such as faint breathing or ticking clocks—that are undetectable through television or monitor speakers.
* **Maintain Consistent Movement Speed:** Avoid sprinting. Sprinting can trigger spatial anomaly triggers late, causing you to cross the threshold before recognizing the hazard.
