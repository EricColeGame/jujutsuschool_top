## Complete Phase Breakdown and Attack Patterns

The battle against the **jujutsu school anomaly final boss** consists of three escalating phases. Each phase introduces new mechanics that limit safe standing zones, introduce status ailments, and force active defensive responses.

```
[Phase 1: 100% - 70% HP] ──> Physical Slams & Linear Void Beams
        │
        ▼
[Phase 2: 70% - 30% HP]  ──> Spatial Distortions & Curse Clones
        │
        ▼
[Phase 3: 30% - 0% HP]   ──> Domain Expansion & Arena Wipe Checks
```

### Phase 1: Physical Pressure and Linear Void Beams (100% to 70% HP)

During the opening phase, the entity stays grounded, utilizing wide horizontal sweeps and devastating forward thrusts. The key to Phase 1 is baiting the heavy ground slam, which leaves the boss vulnerable to counterattacks for roughly three seconds. 

Watch for the boss's right shoulder dipping backward; this signals an upcoming 180-degree cleave. Keep your distance or dodge directly through the attack trajectory using invulnerability frames rather than retreating backward, as the hitbox lingers along the ground.

### Phase 2: Spatial Distortions and Phantom Clones (70% to 30% HP)

Once the boss falls below 70% maximum health, it leaps into the center of the courtyard, creating spatial tears. In this stage of the **jujutsu school anomaly final boss** confrontation, shadow clones spawn around the perimeter while the primary boss unleashes ranged tracking projectiles.

Prioritize eliminating the clones immediately. While their health pools are relatively low, their ranged bind curses can root your character in place, leaving you defenseless against the primary boss's incoming aerial dive.

### Phase 3: Domain Expansion and Enrage Mechanics (30% to 0% HP)

The final phase transforms the arena entirely. The boss constructs an unstable barrier anomaly, blanketing the courtyard in cursed darkness. A permanent damage-over-time aura activates, ticking against all player health bars every two seconds. 

During this phase, the **jujutsu school anomaly final boss** channels a lethal wipe mechanic every 45 seconds. Players must coordinate to strike the floating anomaly sigils situated around the perimeter to shatter the barrier's concentration before the countdown ends.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What level should I be before challenging the jujutsu school anomaly final boss?
Most community reports recommend reaching at least character level 65 with a minimum of Tier IV Technique Mastery before entering the trial. While skilled players can clear the encounter at lower levels, doing so requires exceptional dodge execution and extended fight times due to damage scaling penalties.

### How do I survive the arena-wide wipe attack during the jujutsu school anomaly final boss fight?
When the boss triggers Domain Collapse in Phase 3, quickly locate the glowing resonance sigils around the arena edge. Damaging and destroying these sigils before the 12-second channel concludes interrupts the boss, cancels the lethal attack, and stuns the anomaly for a short damage window.

### Can the boss's attacks be parried or blocked?
Most light physical attacks in Phase 1 can be parried with a well-timed barrier skill. However, purple-tinted void beams, aerial dive slams, and all Phase 3 domain abilities are completely unblockable and must be avoided using dash invulnerability frames or positional movement.

### How many times can I farm the final encounter per week?
Standard reward drops like Shattered Domain Shards and consumable packs can be farmed infinitely. However, Special Grade drops like the Anomaly Heart Core are subject to weekly lockout limits or reduced drop rate diminishing returns depending on active server progression rules.
