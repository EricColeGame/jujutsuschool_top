Entity manifestations are the deadliest category encountered in the game. Unlike static anomalies, cursed entities can track player movement or lurk behind sliding screens. Throughout the academy, yellow paper talismans (*ofuda*) hold back cursed spirits. During Shift 8, look for torn wards, burnt edges on protective strips, or talismans pasted upside down above room lintels. An unsealed doorway creates an instant-death scenario if breached without an anomaly report.
