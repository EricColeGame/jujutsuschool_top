- **Ignoring Acoustic Cues**: Playing without high-fidelity headphones puts you at an extreme disadvantage. Class-B auditory anomalies give away their presence seconds before visual artifacts materialize.

To consistently beat **jujutsu school anomaly shift 12**, maintain a measured pace, verify discrepancies twice before submitting, and never sprint through unchecked doorways.
