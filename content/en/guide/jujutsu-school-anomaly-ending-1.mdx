## Critical Mistakes That Ruin Your Ending 1 Progress

Even veteran players frequently fail their runs due to minute oversights. Understanding why runs fail ensures you do not waste valuable attempts making preventable blunders.

### 1. Second-Guessing Subtle Anomalies
The psychological tension often leads players into "false-positive traps," where they suspect normal geometry is altered due to paranoia. If you turn back in a completely clean hallway, your progress counter drops back to zero. Trust your baseline memory.

### 2. Ignoring Minor Lighting Shifts
A light fixture that is flickering slightly out of rhythm, or a cast shadow stretching in the wrong direction relative to the light source, counts as a full anomaly. Many players look exclusively for monsters and miss simple light placement errors.

### 3. Sprinting Through Thresholds
Running causes your character's head-bobbing animation to obscure peripheral details. Maintain standard walking speed at all times when surveying a new sector.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

To unlock **jujutsu school anomaly ending 1**, player experience indicates you must successfully identify anomalies across 8 to 10 consecutive hallway iterations without a single mistake, culminating in using the Purified Talisman at the moonlit double red doors.

### What happens if I make a mistake during an anomaly inspection?
If you make a mistake—either by moving forward through an anomalous hallway or turning back in a clean hallway—the spatial seal triggers an anomaly reset. Your counter returns to Section 0, requiring you to rebuild your streak from scratch.

### Where do I find the Purified Talisman needed for Ending 1?
The Purified Talisman is located in the school's outdoor courtyard area. Between loop cycles five and six, the exterior sliding door temporarily unlocks. Step outside, locate the small weathered locker underneath the shrine roof eaves, and collect the talisman before returning to the main hallway.

### Can I run through the hallways to speed up my clear time?
