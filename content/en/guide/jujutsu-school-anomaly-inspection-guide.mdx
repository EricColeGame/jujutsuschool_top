## Complete Classification of School Anomalies

Anomalies within the school grounds do not all present themselves in the same manner. Experienced observers categorize irregularities into distinct threat profiles ranging from benign cosmetic glitches to active, lethal pursuers. 

### 1. Visual and Structural Alterations
These are static changes to the school's geometry, signage, or decorations. Common examples include inverted calligraphy scrolls on the shoji screens, extra sliding doors appearing in blank walls, or training weapons shifting angles on wall racks. While non-lethal, they are notoriously easy to overlook when running through areas quickly.

### 2. Environmental and Lighting Distortions
Lighting fluctuations represent some of the most atmospheric deviations. You may encounter blood-red lantern glows, flickering fluorescent tubes, sudden total blackouts, or shadows that cast in the opposite direction of light sources. In certain cycles, the entire corridor may be flooded with an ankle-deep layer of murky water or cursed miasma.

### 3. Auditory Phenomena
Sound anomalies require vigilant active listening. If you hear muffled chanting behind the archive shelves, wet footsteps mirroring your pace, or a ringing school bell during midnight hours, an anomaly is present even if the environment looks pristine.

### 4. Manifestations and Aggressive Entities
The most dangerous anomalies involve manifest cursed entities. You might encounter shadowy figures standing motionless at the end of the hallway, floating masks that turn to watch your character, or hostile stalkers that charge the moment direct line of sight is broken.

## Step-by-Step Zone Inspection Protocols

To achieve consistent completion runs, divide each corridor sweep into four systematic visual checks. Never sprint down the center aisle without completing this sequential scan.

```
Zone 1: Entry Threshold & Ceiling
   ↓
Zone 2: Eye-Level Fixtures (Shoji, Posters, Scrolls)
   ↓
Zone 3: Floorboards, Shadows, & Footsteps
   ↓
Zone 4: Far End Exit Portal & Distant Geometry
```

### Zone 1: Entry Threshold & Ceiling
The moment the sliding door opens into a new sector, halt immediately at the threshold. Look up at the ceiling beams. Cursed schools frequently conceal hanging ropes, reversed support rafters, or weeping talisman seals dangling from rafters. If the ceiling is clear, inspect the immediate light source above the doorway.

### Zone 2: Mid-Level Walls and Calligraphy
Advance three paces into the corridor. Sweep your camera from left to right along the wall hangings. Pay particular attention to:
- The kanji written on protective ward banners (verify stroke directions).
- Noticeboard announcements regarding student curfew.
- Glass frames housing ceremonial wooden daggers.

### Zone 3: Floor Plane and Baseboards
Lower your view angle to inspect the tatami mats and wooden floorboards. Watch for anomalous blood stains, misplaced outdoor sandals (geta), missing shadows beneath furniture, or distorted reflections on polished wood.

### Zone 4: Far-End Perimeter and Exit Door
Before committing to the exit gate, inspect the frame of the door ahead. Check the doorknob height, the presence of sealing cords, and whether the exit sign emits an authentic green glow or an anomalous scarlet flicker.

- **Field of View (FOV):** Set your FOV between 85 and 90. Higher FOVs stretch edge-of-screen textures, making size-based object anomalies harder to evaluate accurately.
- **Brightness / Gamma:** Keep brightness at the developer-recommended default level. Artificial brightening washes out subtle shadow anomalies, rendering inverted shadows invisible.
