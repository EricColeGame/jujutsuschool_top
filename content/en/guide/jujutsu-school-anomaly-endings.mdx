### Ending 1: The Endless Detention (Default Bad Ending)

*The Endless Detention* functions as the primary penalty for failing anomaly checks. Each corridor stretch has a binary state: normal or anomalous. Turning forward while an anomaly exists, or retreating down the stairwell when the corridor is pristine, increments your corruption meter.

* **Trigger Condition:** Make three incorrect directional choices across a single run.
* **Key Visual Tells:** Hallway lights flicker red, lockers violently rattle, and the exit doors lead right back to Floor 1.
* **Outcome:** The player character sits down at an empty desk in classroom 2-B, completely overtaken by creeping shadow vines.

### Ending 2: The Blind Escape (Standard Neutral Ending)

For players who focus strictly on navigating eight consecutive corridors correctly without inspecting environmental lore, *The Blind Escape* is the most common outcome.

* **Trigger Condition:** Reach Loop 8 with 100% directional accuracy while ignoring hidden seals and wall sigils.
* **Crucial Step:** When reaching the final exit doors, exit immediately into the courtyard without checking the principal's display case.
* **Community Notes:** Player experience suggests this route offers the fastest completion time for achievement hunters, clocking in at under seven minutes during optimized runs.

## How to Unlock the Good and True Jujutsu School Anomaly Endings

Unlocking higher-tier outcomes requires thorough interaction with environmental relics rather than simple avoidance. To secure the most coveted of all jujutsu school anomaly endings, you must shift your playstyle from passive evasion to active investigation.

### Achieving Ending 3: Purification Protocol (Good Ending)

To attain the Good Ending, you must dispel high-level curses before taking the main courtyard exit. This requires using the ritual chalk found in the faculty lounge on Loop 4.

1. **Secure the Purifying Chalk:** On Loop 4, check the faculty lounge whiteboard. If no anomaly is present, interact with the chalk tray to store the item.
2. **Identify Floor Sigils:** When navigating Loops 5 through 7, inspect the linoleum floor. Anomalous shadow glyphs must be cleansed by interacting directly with the chalk icon before retreating.
3. **Cleanse Without Mistakes:** Complete all 8 loops without a single failed check.
4. **Final Gate Interaction:** At Loop 8, apply the remaining chalk to the courtyard threshold before opening the gate.

### Unlocking Ending 4: The Exorcist's Awakening (True Ending)

The True Ending reveals the entity responsible for twisting the school's spiritual grounds. It demands absolute perfection and the collection of three concealed paper talismans hidden behind anomalous fixtures.

| Talisman ID | Required Loop | Specific Anomaly Condition | Exact Location |

| **Talisman of Binding** | Loop 5 | Inverted shadow under the trophy shelf | Back panel of the athletic display case |
| **Talisman of Severance** | Loop 7 | Clock hands spinning backward | Inside the shattered classroom wall clock |

Once all three talismans are secured, an ominous ceremonial bell rings when you enter Corridor 8. Instead of the typical courtyard exit, the central hallway splits, revealing the subterranean shrine entrance. Step into the shrine and place the talismans on the stone altar to trigger the true exorcism cutscene. ## Anomaly Detection Strategies and Speedrun Tips

Consistently securing flawless runs across multiple jujutsu school anomaly endings relies on systematic scanning rather than erratic checking. Experienced anomaly hunters divide every corridor into three distinct visual tiers: foreground elements, architectural structures, and audio-spatial cues.

### The Three-Tier Scanning Technique

* **Tier 1 (Immediate Perimeter):** Inspect locker handles, floor tiles, and ceiling fluorescent fixtures within five meters of the spawn point.
* **Tier 2 (Mid-Range Fixtures):** Check room numbers, wall posters, bulletin flyers, and emergency fire extinguishers.
* **Tier 3 (Distance & Audio):** Listen for reversed footsteps, irregular breathing behind walls, and scan the far doorway for subtle silhouette distortions.

Mastering these visual checks prevents costly misidentifications and guarantees clean routing toward your desired narrative climax.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many total jujutsu school anomaly endings can players unlock?

There are five primary jujutsu school anomaly endings currently documented in the game: The Endless Detention (Bad), The Blind Escape (Standard), Purification Protocol (Good), The Exorcist's Awakening (True), and The Forgotten Sorcerer (Secret). Each ending features a unique post-run cutscene and unlocks corresponding achievements in the player profile.

### What happens if I miss an anomaly while trying to get the True Ending?

If you miss an anomaly and proceed forward, your run fails the pristine condition requirement. This resets your talisman count and permanently locks you out of the subterranean shrine for that session, forcing you into either the Bad Ending or the Standard Neutral route.

### Can I unlock the secret ending on my first playthrough?

No. Player experience and game flags confirm that the Midnight Chime event required for *The Forgotten Sorcerer* will not trigger until your local save data contains clearances for both the Standard and Good endings. Complete those prerequisite routes first before hunting for room 3-C's secret scroll.

### What is the most commonly missed anomaly in the school hallways?

The most frequently overlooked anomaly is the delayed mirror reflection in the second-floor washroom window. Because the reflection mirrors your stance accurately for the first two seconds, many players advance past it before realizing the reflection fails to follow their movement when turning away. Always pause for three full seconds when checking reflective surfaces.
