## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a full jujutsu school anomaly walkthrough run take?
A successful, mistake-free run typically takes between 15 to 30 minutes depending on your scouting pace. However, initial blind attempts often require 45 to 60 minutes as players familiarize themselves with the baseline architecture and memorize anomaly spawn points.

### What should I do if an anomaly spawns directly behind me?
If an anomaly spawns at the entrance threshold while you are midway through the corridor, do not panic. Confirm whether the anomaly is an active hostile entity or a static spatial disruption. If an entity blocks the entrance you need to retreat toward, pause out of its aggro range; hostile apparitions will slowly dissipate or follow fixed patrol paths, allowing you to bypass them safely.

### Why did my loop counter reset even though the corridor looked normal?
Subtle anomalies are deliberately easy to miss. The most common missed anomalies include micro-variations such as the emergency exit sign figure running in the opposite direction, clock hands ticking counter-clockwise, or slight changes in ceiling tile textures. If your counter resets, note which sector you just left and re-check those specific subtle elements on subsequent passes.
