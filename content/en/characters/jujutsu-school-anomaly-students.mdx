## Core Archetypes Among Jujutsu School Anomaly Students

Within anomaly response academies, pupils are categorized by combat temperament, curse manipulation style, and anomaly intervention profiles. Understanding these archetypes ensures commanders deploy the right personnel to match unpredictable spatial distortions.

The roster divides into four foundational disciplines: Strikers, Barrier Technicians, Field Observers, and Reversal Specialists. Each discipline dictates how trainees interact with both standard spirits and reality-warping anomalies.

### Vanguard Strikers

Vanguard Strikers engage entities at point-blank range, channeling cursed energy into physical blows to interrupt hostile charge-up sequences. Their role is to establish aggregate threat priority, keeping anomalies pinned down while support units calibrate sealing formations. While durable, Strikers suffer from rapid stamina burn if skirmishes drag on past standard engagement windows.

### Barrier Technicians

Barrier Technicians manipulate boundary arts, establishing simple domains, curtain barriers, and protective perimeters. They serve as the structural anchor of any expedition team. When an anomaly attempts to overwrite regional physics or trap students in pocket zones, Barrier Technicians stabilize surrounding space to buy critical evacuation time.

### Field Observers (Trackers)

Field Observers utilize sensory curse perception to detect anomaly signatures through walls and terrain. Often equipped with secondary tracking tools or shikigami scouts, these students locate breach cores before anomalous entities reach full manifestation.

### Reversal Specialists

Specializing in positive curse energy reversal, these rare students mend physical injuries and purge spiritual corruption from squad members. Because energy reversal demands twice the standard output, these trainees must be shielded from sustained direct attacks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which jujutsu school anomaly students are best for beginners?
Newer players find the most success with Vanguard Strikers and Shikigami Scouts. Vanguards feature forgiving health pools and straightforward melee kits, while Scouts allow players to observe enemy patterns and environmental hazards from safe distances before committing to engagements.

### How do barrier specialists handle sudden environmental anomalies?
Barrier specialists use Simple Spatial Wards to carve out pockets of stabilized reality. When a room shifts or an anomaly attempts to manifest a hazardous domain, deploying a temporary perimeter neutralizes spatial mutations within that specific zone, keeping teammates safe from instant-fail mechanics.

### Can jujutsu school anomaly students clear missions solo?
While technically possible for high-mobility units like the Swift Blade Vanguard, solo runs carry significant risk. Without an Observer to detect hidden reality traps or a Reversal Specialist to purge corruption, a single crowd-control effect can end the excursion instantly.

### What happens when jujutsu school anomaly students run out of cursed energy?
When energy reserves reach zero, trainees enter a vulnerable state called Spiritual Burnout. In this condition, movement speed drops, all special techniques and barriers are disabled, and physical damage resistance is substantially reduced until baseline reserves slowly regenerate.
