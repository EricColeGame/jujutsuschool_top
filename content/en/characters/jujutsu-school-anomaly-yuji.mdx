## Frequently Asked Questions About Jujutsu School Anomaly Yuji

### Why is Yuji considered an anomaly compared to standard Jujutsu High students?
Unlike typical students who awaken inherited clan techniques or standard cursed energy reservoirs, Yuji possessed superhuman physical abilities before encountering cursed spirits. His biological resilience, immunity to lethal cursed poisons, and capacity to retain human consciousness while serving as the vessel for Sukuna make the **jujutsu school anomaly yuji** completely unique within modern jujutsu history.

### Does Yuji Itadori possess an innate cursed technique at enrollment?
No, Yuji enters Jujutsu High without an innate cursed technique. He relies entirely on martial arts, exceptional athletic power, and basic cursed energy reinforcement. Over time, his exposure to Sukuna's presence and further training unlock latent capabilities, including soul-damaging strikes, Divergent Fist, and techniques linked to the Death Painting Wombs.

### How does Divergent Fist work in Yuji's combat style?
Divergent Fist is an accidental combat mechanic born from Yuji's extreme striking speed. Because his physical fist strikes faster than his cursed energy can travel, the target receives two distinct impacts: the initial physical strike followed by a delayed shockwave of cursed energy. While effective against low-tier spirits, Yuji later phased it out against elite fighters in favor of instantaneous, synchronized strikes like the Black Flash.

### Why did the Jujutsu High higher-ups want Yuji executed?
The conservative leadership viewed Yuji as a catastrophe waiting to happen. Normal sorcery rules dictate that any human who consumes a special-grade cursed object like Sukuna's finger is either consumed by poison or transformed into an unstoppable demon. Because the higher-ups could neither control nor predict Yuji's abnormal biology, they deemed him an unacceptable safety risk to society.
