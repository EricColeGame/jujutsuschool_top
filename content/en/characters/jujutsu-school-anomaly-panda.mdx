## Origins and Nature of the Mutated Cursed Corpse

Traditional cursed corpses are synthetic vessels animated by an artificial core injected with cursed energy. Sorcerers routinely use them for training drills, scout duty, and basic utility. However, standard models suffer from a fundamental drawback: they depend on continuous external cursed energy transfusions from their creators to remain active. Without a sorcerer feeding them power, standard puppets eventually run cold and deactivate.

The creation of the jujutsu school anomaly panda challenged the established boundaries set by the jujutsu headquarters. Crafted secretly by Masamichi Yaga—the former Tokyo Prefectural Jujutsu High School principal—Panda is an entirely self-sustaining, sentient mutated cursed corpse. Rather than relying on a solitary, dependent battery, Yaga discovered an unprecedented breakthrough: stabilizing three mutually compatible souls within a single physical form.

By extracting physical soul information from biological beings, duplicating the memories across three independent cores, and placing them inside a single vessel to observe and balance each other, Yaga birthed an autonomous life form. This creature produces its own cursed energy, learns autonomously, and possesses genuine emotional sapience. Consequently, jujutsu elders classified him as an unforeseen biological and metallurgical impossibility.

## Anatomy of the Three Cores: Panda, Gorilla, and Sister

What makes the jujutsu school anomaly panda truly versatile on the battlefield is his proprietary multi-core interior. Instead of collapsing when a vital internal node sustains critical trauma, Panda can shift control among three distinct internal identities, completely altering his physical structure, combat rhythm, and defensive resilience.

Each core embodies an individual persona, combat profile, and energy expenditure model:

### 1. The Default Panda Core (Balance and Endurance)
Panda's primary everyday state emphasizes stability, all-around martial competence, and exceptional blunt-force absorption. In this form, he acts as an exceptional brawler who can deflect heavy strikes using thick synthetic musculature and high durability. His balanced temperament allows him to coordinate tactics with allies and conserve energy during drawn-out encounters.

### 2. The Gorilla Core (Overwhelming Brute Force)
When raw offensive power is required, Panda shifts his main soul driver to his second core: the Gorilla. This transformation visibly thickens his upper body musculature, alters his facial traits, and unlocks devastating offensive properties. His trademark technique in this form—Unblockable Drumming Beat—sends internal shockwaves through physical barriers directly into the opponent's vital organs, completely bypassing conventional surface guarding.

### 3. The Sister Core (High-Speed Defensive Hybrid)
Kept concealed during early engagements, the third core represents his elusive "Sister." Taking the physical thematic persona of a horned triceratops-like brawler, this core delivers high-speed lunges, charging power, and alternate defensive dynamics designed to counter heavy-hitting elemental specialists.

## The Sorcery Regulations Dilemma: Why Higher-Ups Feared Him

To the conservative elders of jujutsu society, the jujutsu school anomaly panda represented a catastrophic paradigm shift. Jujutsu politics revolves around bloodline preservation, clan inheritance, and inherited techniques passed through noble families such as the Gojo, Zen'in, and Kamo clans. 

Panda violated this entire power structure. If a single sorcerer like Principal Yaga possessed the formula to create self-sustaining, sentient cursed corpses equipped with multiple cores, that sorcerer could theoretically mass-produce a standing army of tireless Grade 1 soldiers. Such an army would not require noble ancestry, hereditary cursed techniques, or decades of familial breeding.

Consequently, higher-ups placed Yaga under indefinite monitoring, categorizing Panda's underlying blueprint as an uncontrolled military threat. This fear ultimately culminated in Yaga's tragic execution, underscoring how Panda's very biology served as living evidence of an institutional revolution waiting to happen.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is the character called a jujutsu school anomaly panda?
He is classified as a jujutsu school anomaly panda because, unlike every other cursed corpse developed in the school's history, Panda is fully self-sustaining, self-aware, and powered by three soul cores rather than needing regular cursed energy infusions from an external handler.

### Can Panda regenerate if one of his cores is destroyed?
When a core takes critical damage, it is knocked offline, stripping Panda of the abilities associated with that persona. While his physical frame can be repaired by sewing synthetic material and reinjecting cursed energy, restoring completely obliterated cores requires deep soul-stabilization techniques that were lost following Principal Yaga's demise.

### What makes the Gorilla Core's attacks unblockable?
The Gorilla Core utilizes the Unblockable Drumming Beat technique. Instead of applying impact purely across surface armor, the technique converts cursed kinetic strikes into interior vibrational shockwaves that travel straight through guards into the opponent's internal organs.

### How does the jujutsu school anomaly panda rank compared to other second-year students?
In terms of raw ranking, Panda officially holds a Grade 2 classification. However, with the Gorilla Core activated, his damage output and defensive resilience enable him to comfortably rival Semi-Grade 1 combatants, making him a dependable frontline pillar alongside Maki Zen'in and Toge Inumaki.
